# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Email security@oculix.net with details. Please don't open a public issue
for anything that could be actively exploited before a fix ships.

We'll acknowledge within 5 business days and aim to have a fix or
mitigation plan within 30 days for confirmed issues, faster for anything
critical.

## Scope

This covers chainledger-core itself. It does not cover:
- The hosted chainledger_api service (report those separately, contact TBD)
- Vulnerabilities in dependencies (report upstream, but let us know too)
